电影拍摄构图

Symmetrical Composition 对称构图

Symmetrical composition is when both sides of an image look similar and balanced, like a mirror. It makes the photo look neat, organized, and visually pleasing.

对称构图是指画面左右两边，或上下两边看起来相似、
平衡，就像照镜子那样
这种构图可以让照片整体看起来整齐、有秩序，也给人
一种和谐、舒服的感觉

Overhead 顶视构图

Overhead photography is when the camera takes a picture directly from above the subject, looking straight down. It helps show the arrangement of objects, the surrounding area, or what a person is doing.

顶视构图是指从物体或人物的正上方往下拍摄，就像站在高处往下看
这种角度可以清楚地展示物品的摆放、整体环境或人物正在做的事情

Leading Lines 引导线构图

Leading lines use lines in a photo, such as roads, stairs, railings, or railway tracks, to guide the viewer’s eyes toward the main subject. They create a sense of direction, space, and depth in the image.

引导线构图是利用画面中的线条，例如道路、楼梯、栏杆或铁轨，引导观众的视线看向照片中的重点
这种构图可以让照片更有方向感，也能增加画面的空间感和深度


Low Angle 低角度视角

Low-angle photography is when the camera is placed below the subject and points upward. It can make a person or object look taller, bigger, and more powerful, while creating a dramatic visual effect.

低角度视角是指把相机放在较低的位置，从下往上拍摄人物或物体
这种角度可以让拍摄对象看起来更高大、更有力量感，也能让照片呈现出比较特别/震撼的视觉效果
